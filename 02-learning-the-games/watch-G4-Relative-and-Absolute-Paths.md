## Using this example

John uses MSYS2 on Windows. In that environment, `/c` refers to the Windows C drive, and `/` is the Unix-style root. Your paths may look different. Check your location with `pwd` and put quotation marks around paths that contain spaces.

Copy filenames exactly, including capital letters. Whether your computer treats uppercase and lowercase letters as different depends on its filesystem.
