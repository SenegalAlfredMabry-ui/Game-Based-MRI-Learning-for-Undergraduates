Getting Hands-On with MRI #2: Lesion Identification in Parkinson’s Disease Research Through Brain Image Analysis Reflection Questions
=====================================

Use these questions for written notes or short video reflections. The goal is
to make folder-finding, conversion, ITK-SNAP review, masking, and group
comparison visible to the next beginner.

Suggested notes files:

    nano student_notes/part0_setup_reflection.txt
    nano student_notes/part1_find_study_reflection.txt
    nano student_notes/part2_conversion_reflection.txt
    nano student_notes/part3_itksnap_reflection.txt
    nano student_notes/part4_masking_reflection.txt
    nano student_notes/part5_measure_compare_reflection.txt


Setup checkpoint
----------------

1. What happened when you ran dcm2niix -h?

2. What happened when you ran python3 --version?

3. Did ITK-SNAP open as a regular app?

4. What would you tell a future student to check before they start conversion?


ITK-SNAP image review checkpoint
--------------------------------

1. What subject did you open first?

2. What did the FLAIR help you see?

3. What did the MPRAGE help you see?

4. What display or contrast change made the image easier to inspect?


Masking checkpoint
------------------

1. What subject did you mark first?

2. What label value did you use for lesion-like regions?

3. Where did you save the mask?

4. What was one moment where you had to slow down?


Analysis checkpoint
-------------------

1. What file did the measurement script create?

2. Did any rows say MISSING, NA, or measurement_failed?

3. What did the group comparison say?

4. What would you check first if the group comparison did not make sense?
