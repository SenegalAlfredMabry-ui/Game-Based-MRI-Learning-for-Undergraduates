Round 5 — Laundry Cluster Job
=============================

Goal
----

Submit a fake batch job, check the log, and inspect the outputs.

What this teaches
-----------------

On a real cluster, you often do not run big jobs directly by clicking or typing one command.
You submit a job to a scheduler.

The scheduler runs the job and writes logs.

In this game, scripts/submit_laundry_job.sh is a fake version of that idea.

Dry-cleaner analogy:
Imagine you bring a coat to a dry cleaner. The clerk does not clean it instantly
while you stand there. They take the coat, put it in line behind other people's
items, give you a receipt, and you come back later to check that the job is
finished.

Research analogy:
A cluster works similarly. You submit a job instead of running it directly. The
scheduler puts your job in line, runs it when resources are available, and writes
a log. The log is like a receipt: it tells you what happened, when the job ran,
and whether anything went wrong.

In this round:

    submit_laundry_job.sh = handing the coat to the dry cleaner
    remote_laundromat/jobs = the line of submitted jobs
    remote_laundromat/logs = the receipt or record of what happened
    remote_laundromat/clean_laundry = the finished cleaned items

Step 1 — Put files in the remote incoming folder
------------------------------------------------

Round 5 has its own input folder so it still works even after Round 4 has already
processed the PI Laundromat files.

Run this every time before submitting the job:

    bash scripts/fake_scp.sh dirty_laundry/round5_cluster_job remote_laundromat/incoming

Then check:

    ls remote_laundromat/incoming

Step 2 — Submit the fake job
----------------------------

Run:

    bash scripts/submit_laundry_job.sh

Step 3 — Check the logs
-----------------------

Run:

    ls remote_laundromat/logs

Then open the newest log with cat. For example:

    cat remote_laundromat/logs/latest_job.log

Step 4 — Check the clean remote laundry
---------------------------------------

Run:

    ls remote_laundromat/clean_laundry
    bash scripts/check_remote_laundry.sh
