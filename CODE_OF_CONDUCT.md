# Community expectations

Treat learners and contributors with respect. Discuss ideas and technical problems constructively, recognizing that students bring different experience levels. Harassment, personal attacks, and disclosure of another person’s private information are not acceptable.

For a community concern, use the maintainer contact route. Describe the concern without reposting sensitive material publicly. The project team may edit or remove contributions that violate these expectations and respond in proportion to the concern.
